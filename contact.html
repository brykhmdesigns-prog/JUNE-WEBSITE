Kind questions

Things people often ask

How do I place an order?

Find the piece you love in the shop, add it to your bag, then press “Send order on WhatsApp”. Your message arrives already written, and everything else — price, colours, delivery — is agreed in that chat.

Why are there no prices on the website?

Every piece is made to order, so the price depends on the yarn, the size and the colours you choose. Prices are shared on WhatsApp so you get a clear, honest figure before anything begins.

Can you make something in my size and colours?

Yes — that is the loveliest part of handmade. Share your measurements or the size you usually wear, and the colours you have in mind, and it will be made for you.

How long does a piece take?

It depends on the piece — a beanie is quick, a blanket takes much longer. You will always be given a realistic date before work begins, especially if you need it for a particular day.

How should I care for it?

Hand wash in cool water with a mild soap, squeeze softly rather than wringing, and dry flat in the shade. Keep it folded rather than hung, and it will stay beautiful for years.

Do you deliver outside Kenya?

Pieces travel wherever they are sent. Delivery is arranged on WhatsApp with your location, so you will know the options and the cost before you commit.

Prefer to just talk? That is lovely too.

No forms needed. Send a message on WhatsApp and we will take it from there — quietly, and at your pace.
