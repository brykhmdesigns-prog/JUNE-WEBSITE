The studio

A little studio, a lot of heart

There is no factory here, and no hurry. Each piece is crocheted by hand, one stitch at a time, in colours chosen with care. That is why the collection is small — and why nothing is quite repeated.

Whether it is a bag to carry every day, a top for a warm evening, or a blanket meant to be passed down, everything is started for a person. It is a slow way of making things, and a lovely one.
